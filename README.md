# Lightshow

Old-school music visuals that dance to whatever your computer is playing.

**Try it:** https://lightshow.juriaan.design

Press Space, choose "Entire screen" and switch on "Also share system audio" (Chrome or Edge on a computer). Sharing a single tab with its audio works too, but the browser then shows a "Sharing" bar over the picture. The Microphone button works everywhere.

Everything runs in the browser; nothing is uploaded. The whole app is `index.html`.
